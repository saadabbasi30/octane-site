04 — Warning signs

When to bring it in.

Catching a problem early usually means a smaller, cheaper repair.

A

A warning light comes on

Check engine, battery, oil or brake lights mean your car needs attention. A flashing check-engine light is urgent.

B

Squealing or grinding brakes

Noise when stopping usually means worn pads. The sooner, the cheaper.

C

Temperature gauge climbing

Overheating can damage an engine fast. Pull over safely and call.

D

Spots under the car

Oil, coolant or transmission fluid leaks are worth a quick look.

E

Shaking or rough idle

Vibration, stalling or hesitation can point to engine or suspension issues.

F

Slow or clicking starts

A tired battery or starter usually gives warning before it quits.
